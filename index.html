隐私政策 · Privacy Policy

Luck is coming　｜　最近更新：2026 年 10 月 / Last updated: October 2026

「Luck is coming」是一款陪伴与灵感类的小应用。我们不把你的数据当作资产，也不做任何窥探。这份政策用最直白的话告诉你：我们拿了什么、怎么用、以及你随时能拿回什么。

一、我们不收集你的个人信息
  • 本应用无需注册、无需登录，我们不要求你提供姓名、手机号、邮箱或任何账号。
  • 你写下的一句句心愿、每次记录的心情，都只保存在你自己的设备本地（使用系统自带的本地数据库）。
  • 这些数据不会上传到任何服务器，开发者（我们）也无法读取、也无法看到它们。
  • 本应用不申请相册、相机、麦克风、定位、通讯录等任何系统权限，也没有分享或导出功能；你写下的内容不会被本应用发送到任何地方。
二、我们没有第三方追踪
  • 本应用不含任何广告 SDK、不含任何第三方数据分析 SDK。
  • 我们不会追踪你的行为，不会生成用户画像，也不会把任何信息卖给第三方。
三、关于订阅与支付

本应用提供可选的「Luck is coming Pro」自动续费订阅。订阅通过 Apple App Store 完成，与你 Apple ID 绑定的支付方式结算。

  • 支付过程由 Apple 直接处理，我们不收集、不存储你的任何支付信息（如银行卡号、密码等）。
  • 我们仅接收 Apple 提供的必要订阅状态（如是否已订阅），用于解锁对应功能，不涉及你的具体支付账户。
  • 你可以随时在 iPhone「设置 → 你的名字 → 订阅」中管理或取消。订阅会在当前周期到期前 24 小时自动续费，关闭自动续费后将不再扣款。
四、关于儿童

本应用内容温和、正向，不含不适宜未成年人观看的信息。但我们建议在监护人指导下使用，尤其低龄儿童。

五、你如何删除自己的数据

心愿、日记、星河记录都只存在你这台设备上；卸载本应用即删除这些内容，无需额外操作。为防重复领取，免费次数的计数保存在系统钥匙串里，不含你写下的任何内容。若开启了 iCloud 或电脑整机备份，旧备份里可能仍有一份副本，可在系统「设置 → 你的名字 → iCloud → 管理储存空间」或电脑上删除。

六、本政策的更新

如本政策有调整，我们会在应用内与本页面同步更新。你继续使用即表示接受更新后的政策。

七、联系我们

如你对隐私有任何疑问，欢迎邮件联系我们：846286617@qq.com

Luck is coming is a small companion and inspiration app. We don't treat your data as an asset, and we don't pry. This policy tells you in plain words: what we take, how we use it, and how you can take it back anytime.

1. We Don't Collect Your Personal Information
  • No registration or login required. We don't ask for your name, phone number, email, or any account.
  • The wishes you write and the moods you record are stored only on your own device, using the system's built-in local database.
  • This data is never uploaded to any server, and we (the developers) cannot read or see it.
  • The app asks for no system permissions—no Photos, camera, microphone, location, or contacts—and has no sharing or export features. Nothing you write is sent anywhere by the app.
2. No Third-Party Tracking
  • This app contains no advertising SDK and no third-party analytics SDK.
  • We don't track your behavior, build user profiles, or sell any information to third parties.
3. About Subscriptions & Payments

This app offers an optional "Luck is coming Pro" auto-renewing subscription, handled through the Apple App Store and settled with the payment method linked to your Apple ID.

  • Payments are processed directly by Apple. We don't collect or store any of your payment information (such as card numbers or passwords).
  • We only receive the necessary subscription status from Apple (such as whether you're subscribed), used solely to unlock features—it never involves your specific payment account.
  • You can manage or cancel anytime in iPhone Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. Subscriptions auto-renew 24 hours before the period ends; turning off auto-renew stops future charges.
4. About Children

This app's content is gentle and positive, with nothing unsuitable for minors. We still recommend use under guardian supervision, especially for young children.

5. How to Delete Your Data

Your wishes, diary, and star-river records live only on this device; uninstalling the app removes them—no extra steps. To prevent reclaiming free daily uses, a small counter is kept in the system Keychain; it holds none of your written content. If you use iCloud or a computer backup, older backups may still contain a copy; you can delete them in Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage, or on your computer.

6. Policy Updates

If this policy changes, we'll update it in the app and on this page. Continued use means you accept the updated policy.
